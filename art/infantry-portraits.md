# Infantry portrait direction

Generated using the built-in image-generation tool. Original outputs remain in
Codex generated_images; optimized project assets are in
`public/assets/ui/command-icons/{infantry,sniper,grenadier,rocket-infantry}-portrait.webp`.
Encoded at 512px wide with WebP quality 88, preserving the original aspect ratio.

Shared prompt direction: premium realistic military RTS selection portrait,
landscape 4:3; waist-up adult commando with believable human anatomy, olive/forest
green equipment and charcoal tactical armor, navy smoky studio background,
warm amber key light and cool cyan rim light. Face and distinctive equipment
large and readable at small card sizes. No text, logos, UI, gore, voxel geometry,
cartoon, or low-poly treatment. The first rifleman portrait was used as the
style reference for the remaining three images.

Role-specific final prompt directions:

- Rifle Team: helmet with goggles above eyes, compact assault rifle diagonally
  across chest, alert three-quarter stance, gloved hands and visible face.
- Sniper: olive hood with sparse ghillie camouflage, subtle face camouflage,
  precision rifle with a large telescopic scope and long barrel, calm focus.
- Grenadier: rugged commando, visible 40mm ammunition bandolier and standalone
  six-cylinder drum grenade launcher, confident forward stance.
- Rocket Team: substantial flak vest, radio pack and antenna, thick anti-armor
  launcher braced on the shoulder, visible face beside the launcher.

These are illustrative portraits. No battlefield models or simulation rules change.
