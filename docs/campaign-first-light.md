# Operation First Light — mission script

Opening mission for Israel. The opening briefing uses Dani’s supplied ElevenLabs recording (`first-light-briefing.mp3`), copied without re-encoding. It plays once at mission start, continues across the first-strike transition, and supports replay, mute and dismissal. The visible briefing matches the approved script. The Iron Dome follow-up uses Dani’s second recording (`first-light-shield-online.mp3`). It plays once after a completed battery is powered; if the opening is still playing, it waits for the opening to finish. Both transmissions support replay, mute and dismissal. The victory update remains text-only.

## Mission flow

- Begin with a supplied, operational Israeli base, walls, defensive guns, workshops, troops and 6,500 credits. No Iron Dome is prebuilt.
- At 24 seconds, the first Iranian cell launches; the other fires three seconds later. Both use actual strategic-missile/interception mechanics.
- The Defense tab opens and the Iron Dome card is highlighted until a battery is built.
- Launch cells fire again every 65 seconds while alive. Scout, produce a strike force and destroy both.
- Victory requires both launchers destroyed with Command HQ intact; surviving guards do not block completion.
- Mission state and launch timing persist in saves. Normal AI base construction is disabled for this authored mission.

## Lieutenant Yael — recorded opening briefing

This is Lieutenant Yael, your liaison at Northern Command. We’ve detected missiles incoming from two Iranian launch sites. Our base has defenses, but no Iron Dome coverage. Your first priority is to build and power an Iron Dome battery. Then scout the marked areas, assemble your strike force, and destroy both launchers. Keep Command HQ standing. Find those launchers and take them out.

## Iron Dome online

Yael here. Iron Dome is online, but it won’t stop every missile. Keep your defenses powered while we prepare a strike.

Check your credits and make sure your ore collector is working. If income is slow, strengthen your economy before spending everything on aircraft.

Build a small strike force, then scout the two marked areas. Each launcher has guards—use your aircraft to spot them and plan your approach.

Destroy both missile launchers to end the attacks. Protect the base, but keep moving toward the objective.

## The sky is clear

Both launchers are destroyed. No further launches are detected. Command HQ is secure. Well done, Commander. The base is safe. Operation First Light is complete.
