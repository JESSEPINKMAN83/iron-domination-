# USA faction local trial

Select **USA · Heavy Strike** in skirmish setup. The new doctrine ID is `expeditionary-force`; existing Israel, Iran and Ukraine saves and choices remain supported. Multiplayer room settings preserve this fourth doctrine too.

## Roster and tradeoffs

The US roster uses American infantry, Stryker, Abrams, Paladin, M777, Viper, Apache and Reaper identities with static cinematic portraits. Four additional platforms broaden the conventional roster:

| Platform | Cost | Build time | Gameplay role |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| M2 Bradley IFV | 750 | 14 s | Tracked autocannon support with a guided anti-armor secondary |
| M270 MLRS | 1,450 | 22 s | Three staggered rockets, 60 minimum / 290 maximum range, 9 s reload |
| F-15E Strike Eagle | 1,900 | 26 s | Fast ground-strike jet with a separate air-to-air secondary |
| B-2 Spirit | 3,200 | 40 s | Two heavy conventional bombs, 175 range including V mode, 12 s reload; no anti-air weapon |

All costs, health, ranges, speeds and payloads are **RTS balancing choices**, not real equipment specifications. Bombers turn broadly and need escorts. Specialist replacements are expensive and slow. US defenses use Patriot and C-RAM identities; this faction cannot build Arrow 3 or the Iranian strategic missile program. Nothing here implies real American air defenses are inherently poor.

Models use shared static geometry and existing animation systems. New F-15E/B-2 models are tested at no more than five meshes and fewer than 350 triangles each. Thumbnail files are static WebP assets; instructor images load through the existing guide. This is a geometry budget check, not an FPS guarantee on all hardware.
